# ⏰ Time Management — Exam Day Strategy

> Paper exams are won or lost by time allocation. Plan before you write.

---

## 🎯 The Golden Rule

**Before the exam starts:**
1. Scan ALL questions in the first 2 minutes
2. Mark: Easy (E), Medium (M), Hard (H)
3. Do all E questions first, then M, then H
4. Allocate time by points, not by question number

---

## ⏱️ Time Allocation Template

Assuming a 2-hour exam with ~100 points:

| Phase | Time | Activity |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Survey** | 5 min | Read all questions, note difficulty and point values |
| **Easy wins** | 30 min | Answer all questions you know confidently |
| **Medium questions** | 45 min | Tackle questions you know partially |
| **Hard questions** | 30 min | Attempt anything you can on remaining questions |
| **Buffer/Review** | 10 min | Review answers, check calculations |

**Rule:** 1 point ≈ 1 minute. A 20-point question deserves 20 minutes.

---

## 🔥 During the Exam

### When you're stuck:
1. **Spend 30 seconds max** trying to recall
2. **Write what you know** — partial credit!
3. **Skip and return** — don't let one hard question eat your time
4. **Mark it** so you remember to return

### When running out of time:
1. **Show formulas** — even without calculating, the formula is worth points
2. **Write brief outlines** — "Step 1: Calculate AMAT using... Step 2: Apply to CPI..."
3. **Do easy parts of hard questions** — "a)" might be trivial even if "d)" is hard

---

## 📐 Point-Maximizing Rules

### Rule 1: Easy parts first
Every question has easier sub-parts. In a 5-part question, answer a, b, c before spending time on d, e.

### Rule 2: Show all work
`Answer: 42` = risky (all-or-nothing)
`AMAT = 4 + 0.08 × 100 = 4 + 8 = 12 cycles` = safe (each step earns points)

### Rule 3: Attempt everything
A blank answer earns 0. An attempted answer earns partial credit.

### Rule 4: Check units
"12" vs "12 cycles" — units often cost a point.

### Rule 5: Define acronyms
Write TLB = Translation Lookaside Buffer once. Then use TLB freely.

## 🏆 Question Priority by Topic (Estimated Frequency)

**Answer immediately if you see these — they're predictable:**
- AMAT calculation ← almost guaranteed
- Pipeline timing diagram ← very common
- Address breakdown (tag/index/offset) ← very common
- Amdahl's Law calculation ← very common
- Virtual address translation ← common
- CPI calculation ← common

**These may appear — be prepared:**
- Write policy comparison (write-through vs write-back)
- Branch prediction state diagram
- Pipeline hazard identification
- TLB vs page table comparison
- Interrupt vs exception distinction

---

## 💡 Before You Leave

With last 5 minutes:
- [ ] Every question has at least something written
- [ ] All calculations show the formula
- [ ] All answers include units
- [ ] Your name is on every page
- [ ] Multi-part questions: check you answered all parts (a, b, c...)

---

## 🧠 Mental Game

- **You've prepared** — trust it
- **Partial credit exists** — don't panic over imperfect answers
- **Breathe** — if stuck, take 10 seconds to breathe before re-reading
- **Move on** — don't spiral on one question
- **You know more than you think** — start writing, the words come
